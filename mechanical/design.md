# Mechanical Design

The robot was designed as a compact two-wheeled self-balancing platform.

## Design Considerations

- Lightweight and stable chassis
- Proper wheel and motor placement
- Suitable center of gravity for balancing
- Compact arrangement of electronics
- Easy access for maintenance and testing
- Adequate structural support for the electronics and motors

## Chassis

The chassis was designed to provide structural support for the motors, wheels, battery, sensors, and control electronics.

Mechanical design was explored using CAD tools, including SolidWorks.

## Wheels and Motors

The robot uses two driven wheels. The motors provide both movement and the rapid corrective movements required for maintaining balance.

## Electronics Placement

The electronics were arranged on the robot while considering:

- Weight distribution
- Center of gravity
- Sensor positioning
- Motor-driver accessibility
- Battery placement
- Protection of electronic components

## Development

The mechanical and electronic design went through testing and modification during the development process.

The project involved troubleshooting and replacing components as different hardware issues were encountered.
